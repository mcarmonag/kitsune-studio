## AI Lab demos

### EU AI Act risk classifier · [Try it](https://kitsune-studio.eu/eu-ai-act)
An interactive questionnaire that classifies an AI use case into the EU AI Act risk levels (prohibited, high, limited or minimal risk) and explains the obligations and application dates for providers and deployers.

- Follows the order in which the Act is applied: prohibited practices → regulated products (Annex I) → high-risk areas (Annex III) with the Article 6(3) exception → transparency (Article 50)
- Includes the changes introduced by the Digital Omnibus on AI (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744)
- Bilingual (Spanish / English), with built-in examples
- Runs entirely in the browser: no data leaves your device

*Educational demo, not legal advice.*
